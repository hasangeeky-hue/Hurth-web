The university application is one of the most important stages on the way to studying in Germany, and one of the most critical. Applying looks as easy as collecting offer letters. Once you start, the questions arrive: what is uni-assist, what is a VPD, how do you send your documents to Germany? And even if you know the process, applying at random and collecting an offer letter will not help you in the long run.

What we do in this stage

With a team behind you, the journey is calmer. Our team has been working through cases like yours in our Facebook community since 2020. Together we:

  • find the right course and the right university for your profile;
  • write your CV and your motivation letter;
  • calculate your grade precisely on the German scale for the VPD;
  • arrange notary support and send your documents to Germany by DHL.

Nothing happens behind your back

This stage includes four video consultations. We process your application in front of you, so you see the progress as it happens, and you can open your file in your portal at any time. Nothing is hidden.
