Since then we have worked through many difficult cases there, and the people who solve them are students at German universities themselves. We built the team that way so we can handle whatever situation comes up and help you reach your goal.

Pay for the stage you need

Many students who go to an agency pay a large sum, and too often they are let down. Agencies also tend to sell one bundle instead of the help you actually need.

Aquariende is an online consultancy where you choose the service that fits your situation. If you need help only until your offer letter, take that stage. If you need help only with the visa application, take that one. Our service starts with a profile check and ends with the visa application, the last step before you study abroad.
