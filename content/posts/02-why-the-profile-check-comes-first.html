Imagine you have applied to several courses and paid for the applications, and then it turns out your grade conversion (VPD) is not good enough for any of them. No offer letter arrives, and the money and the months are gone.

A case we have seen

One student booked an embassy appointment without knowing what profile German universities expect or how to apply. Eight or nine months later they still had no offer letter. When the embassy asked for their documents, they had nothing to submit, and they were blocked from the embassy for six months.

That is what happens when you act before you know the process. Embassy rules and waiting times change, so read this as an example from 2022, not as a rule.

What the profile check does

This is why we start with a mandatory profile check. We review your CV and your certificates and look for universities whose programmes relate to what you studied before. Then you apply where you have a real chance of an offer letter.
