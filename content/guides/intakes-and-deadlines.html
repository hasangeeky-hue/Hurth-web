Two semesters a year

German universities run a winter semester and a summer semester. Not every programme starts in both: the programme's own page says when you can begin.

Deadlines are set per programme

There is no national deadline. Each university sets its own, and one university can have different deadlines for different programmes, for programmes with and without admission restrictions, and for applicants with German or foreign qualifications.

Planning your year, without dates

  1. Check eligibility first: your degree in anabin and your converted grade.
  2. Make a deadline list for every programme on your shortlist, from each programme's own page.
  3. Request VPDs early, as soon as you know which universities use them.
  4. Apply and collect admission letters. You need one to register for the visa.
  5. Prepare your financial proof and visa documents while you wait.
  6. Apply for the visa within the window the embassy allows before your arrival, and talk to your university early if the semester may start before your visa arrives.
