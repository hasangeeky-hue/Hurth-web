How much?

[aq_volatile topic="blocked-account"]

The Federal Foreign Office explains that the amount depends on the purpose of your stay and is based on the maximum support rates for students in Germany. It sends you to the German mission's website or the Consular Services Portal for the current figure. A scholarship can reduce the amount you must deposit, because the embassy credits it to your means of living.

How a blocked account works

  • It is a bank account in Germany in your name, usually opened online with a bank or another provider.
  • Only a set amount can be withdrawn each month.
  • The mission abroad or the foreigners authority is named on the account, and it cannot withdraw the money.
  • It is usually opened for one year, unless your stay is shorter.

The other kinds of financial proof

The German Embassy Dhaka accepts three kinds of proof that you can pay for your living costs:

  1. a scholarship or stipend confirmation;
  2. a formal sponsorship declaration (Verpflichtungserklärung) from a sponsor living in Germany;
  3. a blocked account in Germany in your name.

A bank account of your parents in Bangladesh is not on this list.

Which provider?

We recommend none. The Federal Foreign Office recommends no provider either, accepts no liability for providers, and deleted the list of providers it used to publish in 2022. It advises you to choose carefully after your own research.
