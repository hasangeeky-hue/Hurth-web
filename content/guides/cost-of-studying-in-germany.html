Why there is no single number

Students in Bangladesh often search for one number: how much money it takes to go to Germany. There is no honest single answer: each cost is set by a different body, several depend on your programme or your city, and most change every year. Tuition: generally none at state universities

Most universities in Germany are state-funded. Bachelor's programmes and most master's programmes there generally charge no tuition. There are exceptions: Baden-Württemberg charges students from outside the EU, universities in Bavaria may charge them, some continuing-education master's programmes charge fees, and private universities may charge much more.

The semester contribution is not tuition. Every student pays it; it covers student services and the student government and often includes a public transport ticket.
