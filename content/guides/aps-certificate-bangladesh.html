What the embassy says

In its student visa FAQ (dated 11 January 2026), the German Embassy Dhaka states that it does not use an APS office, and that applicants should not apply through another country's APS office. As of 21 November 2026, when we last checked, that was still its answer.

What APS is

APS (Akademische Prüfstelle) is an office that checks applicants' academic documents for German study, in countries that have one. APS India, for example, is part of the German Embassy in New Delhi, works with DAAD and was set up in 2022. In Vietnam, the APS is part of the German Embassy in Hanoi and checks whether documents are genuine and whether applicants meet the admission criteria.

What this means for you

You do not get an APS certificate in Bangladesh for the visa. Your documents are still checked: universities and uni-assist evaluate them, and the embassy states that any attempt to submit fake or falsified documents is subject to penalties.
