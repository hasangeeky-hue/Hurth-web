The checklist

  1. A degree from a recognised institution. Look up your university in anabin and check its status.
  2. A grade that meets the programme's minimum, after conversion to the German scale.
  3. Proof of the teaching language: English or German, as the programme asks.
  4. A fitting subject. The programme's page says which earlier studies it accepts.
  5. Sometimes a VPD from uni-assist, if the university uses that procedure.

Each programme sets its own rules. Read its admission page first; everything below helps you read it.

Is your university recognised? anabin

anabin is the database of the German education ministers' Central Office for Foreign Education (ZAB). Search for your institution and open its status: H+ means it is a state or state-recognised institution; H+/- means additional criteria apply. The database is not complete, so an institution may be missing or not yet rated. In that case, ask the university you are applying to.

Your grade on the German scale

Your CGPA is converted with the modified Bavarian formula. The university, or uni-assist inside a VPD, makes the conversion that counts; online converters only give an estimate. [aq_link key="guide-grade"]How the conversion works, with an example[/aq_link].

English or German

The programme decides which language proof it accepts. For the visa, the German Embassy Dhaka lists TOEFL or IELTS for English-taught programmes, unless your bachelor's degree is from Australia, the UK or the US. For German-taught programmes it recommends a B1 certificate with a good result. Language tests must be taken in person: the embassy states that online certificates are generally not accepted.

To find programmes taught in English, use the DAAD database of international programmes and filter by course language.

uni-assist and the VPD

Only some universities use uni-assist's VPD. If yours does, request it early: uni-assist advises at least 8 weeks before the deadline. [aq_link key="guide-vpd"]uni-assist and the VPD, explained[/aq_link].
