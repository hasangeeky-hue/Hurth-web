The embassy's PDF is the only complete list. Open the German Embassy Dhaka's student visa checklist and follow it item by item. The summary below helps you prepare; it does not replace it.

What the embassy asks for, in summary

  • A passport issued within the last 10 years, with at least two empty pages
  • One biometric photo, not older than 6 months
  • The visa application form and the declaration, signed
  • Your proof of admission (and your scholarship or PhD letter, if you have one)
  • Your higher secondary certificate and mark sheets, and proof of earlier studies
  • Proof of language: TOEFL or IELTS for English-taught programmes, unless your bachelor's degree is from Australia, the UK or the US; for German-taught programmes a B1 certificate with a good result is recommended
  • A motivation letter that answers the embassy's questions about your studies and your plans
  • Your CV, including any employment
  • Travel health insurance valid from your arrival in Germany
  • Financial proof: a scholarship confirmation, a formal sponsorship declaration or a blocked account
  • The visa fee, in cash

Mistakes the embassy names

  • Two language versions. Do not submit documents in both English and German. The embassy prefers the German version.
  • Untranslated documents. Documents in Bengali need an English or German translation.
  • Online language tests. Certificates must be obtained in person; online certificates are generally not accepted.
  • Arriving late. More than 15 minutes late means a new appointment.
  • Two applications at once. A Schengen visa and a national visa cannot be processed at the same time.
  • The wrong embassy. You can apply in Dhaka only if you live permanently in the embassy's area of jurisdiction.
  • Fake documents. Any attempt to submit fake or falsified documents is subject to penalties.
