What a VPD is

VPD stands for Vorprüfungsdokumentation, a preliminary review documentation. It is a certificate from uni-assist that you submit directly to the university when you apply there. It shows which certificates you submitted, how uni-assist evaluates them and how it rates your grade on the German grading system.

When you need one

Only some universities that work with uni-assist use the VPD procedure. Check the university's own application page, or ask it; it can also tell you which documents to upload to uni-assist. A VPD is valid for one year from the date it is issued.

The steps

  1. Find out from the university whether it wants a VPD.
  2. Create your uni-assist account with your own email address.
  3. Upload your documents and submit the online application for the VPD.
  4. Pay the fee through uni-assist's own portal only.
  5. When the VPD arrives, upload it to the university's own application before its deadline.

Fake affiliation: a warning

uni-assist states that it does not work with commercial agencies, that some organisations falsely claim to be affiliated with it, and that payment requests come only from uni-assist through its own portal. DAAD likewise warns about agencies that pretend to represent German universities.
