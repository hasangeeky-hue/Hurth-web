Why it comes first

Applications cost money and time. Before you submit one, you should know whether you have a chance of being accepted under the requirements of the German education system. Our service starts here. We call it "mandatory" because it is our first step, not because any authority requires it.

What we look at

Before your call, the consultant who owns your case reviews your documents and rates six areas, each as strong, needs work or a blocker:

  1. Your grade, converted to the German scale
  2. Your English, against what your target programmes ask for
  3. How well your subject fits the programmes you want
  4. Your study gap, if you have one, and how you explain it
  5. Your funds for the visa
  6. Your documents

What you get

  • A 30-minute video consultation with a consultant who studies in Germany
  • CV checking and document evaluation
  • Suggestions of universities related to your previous study
  • A written report in your portal with the rating of each area and what to fix

What to prepare

You upload your documents in your portal before you book the consultation: your CV, SSC and HSC certificates, your bachelor's certificate and transcript if you have them, your English test result if you have one, and your passport.

What happens after

If your profile is ready, the next step is the [aq_service_link stage="application"]university application service[/aq_service_link], and only if you want it. If it is not ready yet, the report tells you why and what to work on.

What it is not

The profile check is an honest assessment, not a promise. It does not give a chance in per cent, and no one can say in advance whether a university will admit you.
