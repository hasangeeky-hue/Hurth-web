How you get your portal link

Your portal account is created when you pay the consultation fee, and the link comes to you by email. Nothing is paid on this website, and no link to the portal is sent before you have decided to start.

Keep your logins safe

If a message asks you to pay or to log in somewhere and you are not sure it comes from us, ask us on WhatsApp first. Never share your Consular Services Portal login with anyone: the German Embassy Dhaka warns about agents misusing applicants' data.
