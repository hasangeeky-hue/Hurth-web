What we prepare with you

  • Your file, checked against the German Embassy Dhaka's official checklist
  • Opening the blocked account, if that is your financial proof
  • Filling out the visa form properly
  • Your letter of intent for the visa application
  • Deferring your semester according to the visa timeline, if your visa may come late
  • Travel insurance, your air ticket and a local transport ticket in Germany
  • Three video consultations and a mock appointment, so you know what to expect

What only you can do

You register yourself in the Consular Services Portal once you hold a valid admission letter. You attend the appointment yourself: at VFS if you are a master's applicant, at the embassy if you are a bachelor's applicant. The visa fee is paid there, in cash, in taka.

The embassy encourages applicants to apply on their own and warns about agents who cancel appointments without telling their clients. Keep your portal login to yourself.

What no one can promise

No one can guarantee a visa. The German Embassy says so itself: no agency can guarantee that a visa will be approved. Every application is decided by the embassy's staff; VFS only receives documents.

Blocked account providers

We recommend no provider. The Federal Foreign Office recommends none either, and it deleted its old list of providers in 2022.
