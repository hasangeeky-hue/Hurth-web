The steps, in plain words

Your child first checks whether their degree and grade meet a German programme's rules, then applies to universities. With an admission letter, they apply for a national student visa at the German Embassy Dhaka. For the visa they must show how they will pay for their living costs, usually with a blocked account in Germany in their own name. What it costs

There is no single total, because each cost is set by a different body and changes. Tuition is generally not charged at state universities for bachelor's and most master's programmes, with exceptions. Every student pays a semester contribution. The visa fee is paid in cash at the appointment and is not refunded if the visa is refused. [aq_link key="guide-cost"]Every cost category, with the official page for each[/aq_link].

How to recognise a careful consultant

The German Embassy Dhaka encourages students to apply on their own and warns about agents who make false promises or cancel appointments. DAAD warns about agencies that falsely claim to represent German universities. Whoever helps your child, the applications should stay in your child's own name and email, and no one can promise a visa.
