Our story

Our mission started in 2020, when the COVID-19 pandemic began and the normal way of getting a visa and entering Germany was disrupted. We opened a Facebook group to anybody who wanted to pursue higher education and get support for free, and we answered students' cases there.

From that group came the consultation we offer today, for students from Bangladesh who want to study at German universities: from checking a profile to preparing the visa file. Everything happens online, so a student can complete the admission file from anywhere.

Why we work this way

The German Embassy Dhaka and DAAD both warn about agents who make false promises, charge high fees or claim to represent universities. We built our process the other way round: your applications are in your name, you approve each step, you can see your file, and we say before you pay that no one can guarantee a visa.
