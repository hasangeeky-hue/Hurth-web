How we work on your applications

  1. A shortlist you approve. We find subjects and universities that fit your profile and agree the list with you before anything is submitted.
  2. Accounts in your own email. Your uni-assist and university accounts are created with your email address, and you can see the login details.
  3. The VPD starts early. Where a university uses uni-assist's VPD, we start it right after the shortlist, because it takes weeks. We calculate your grade carefully for it.
  4. Your CV and motivation letters, from your own story. You approve each one.
  5. Paper only where it is needed. Notary and DHL support for the programmes that ask for certified copies by post.
  6. Nothing is submitted without your approval. Each application is sent only after you have approved it.
  7. You see every step. Your tracker in the portal shows the state of each application.

What is included

  • Finding your subject and universities
  • A standard CV
  • Motivation letters
  • VPD support and uni-assist application support
  • Notary support and DHL support
  • Four video consultations

What stays with you

You pay uni-assist and the universities directly. uni-assist states that its payment requests come only from uni-assist and are processed only through its own online portal.

What we cannot promise

Each university decides on its own applications. We cannot promise an offer, and we have no partnership with any university.
