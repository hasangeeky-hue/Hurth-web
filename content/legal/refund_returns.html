Overview

Our refund policy follows the terms and conditions we have stated.

  • For processing purposes, we will keep your information for a full year. After that, it will be removed in accordance with data privacy regulations.
  • You have agreed to give your data to us.
  • You have an obligation to us to submit only authentic required documents to the institution.
  • If you submit any forged or altered papers during the application process, we will not reimburse your service charge.
  • This service requires a signed agreement for full disclosure.
  • If we make an error processing your application, we will reimburse 75% of your money (less our service fee of 25%) within 15 days.
  • That money will be refunded through the aamarPay payment gateway.
  • Before refunding money, our team will scrutinize the facts and other issues.
  • During the consultancy period, you have to give us access to your “Email, uni-assist, and university login account”.
  • During the consultancy, the customer cannot take advice from other consultants or third parties. If the customer is involved in this kind of activity, we will not reimburse the service charge.
  • This is a consultancy service. For the mandatory profile check, there is no refund policy; it is a one-time payment.
  • For the application to the university service: once you get your offer letter from the university, it is non-refundable.
  • The customer needs to sign an acknowledgment paper of receiving the services.
  • The same applies to the visa procedure services.
  • The customer cannot ask for a refund in the middle of the consultation, and cannot cancel the services in the middle of the consultation.
  • Customers can only cancel from 7 days before the service start time to 7 days after.
